Project Overview

A Vision for Dining in Iowa

De Boas Brazilian Steakhouse is a full-scale two-story restaurant concept designed from the ground up as an architecture and engineering capstone. Located near Target on Ashworth Road in Waukee, Iowa, the project spans site selection, schematic design, structural analysis, code compliance, and interior rendering — all developed in Revit.

The name De Boas — Portuguese for "feeling good" — reflects the warm, communal spirit of Brazilian churrasco culture, elevated to an upscale dining experience that meets Waukee's building codes while delivering a distinctive, thematic atmosphere.

Regulatory Compliance

Building Code Summary

The project was developed in full compliance with applicable IBC, IMC, NEC, ADA, and Iowa DNR codes — documented and cross-referenced in Phase III to ensure design integrity across accessibility, mechanical, and site requirements.

Accessibility & Egress

ADA 2010 §404 Min. 36" accessible entry doors with lever hardware
IBC §1006 Step-free entrance via accessible ramp, max 1:12 slope
ADA §206 Continuous accessible route: parking → entry → dining → restrooms → egress
ADA §208 1 van-accessible space per 25 parking spaces; 7-ft ADA aisle
ADA §213 ADA-compliant restroom per floor; 1 accessible stall minimum

Mechanical & Electrical

IMC §403 Ventilation per A-2 occupancy rates; outdoor air circulation for full building
IMC §507.2 Type I exhaust hood: grease ducts, filters, integrated suppression in kitchen
NEC §2702 Adequate branch circuits; lighting, emergency standby power for egress
NFPA §7.9 90-min battery backup emergency lighting throughout
IBC §1014 Nonslip flooring (COF ≥ 0.6) in kitchen; handrails on all stairs

Project Site

The Location

Situated along the Ashworth Road corridor in Waukee, Iowa — selected for its high-visibility commercial surroundings, proximity to retail traffic, and available acreage to accommodate the building footprint, surface parking, and required riparian buffer.
